# Mechatronic Positioning System for Fringe Projection Shape Measurement Device

## Project Overview
Development of a next-generation mechatronic positioning system for a shape measurement device (fringe projector) utilized in medical and industrial applications. 

This project simulates a real-world engineering environment, consisting of a principal investigator (professor) and a development team of 20 engineers (students) operating under a corporate framework to deliver a finalized, test-ready design.

---

## Technical Approach & Methodology

### 1. Background & Application Requirements
* **Industrial Application:** High-precision coordinate capture, surface defect inspection, and metrology under varying thermal and mechanical conditions.
* **Medical Application:** Rapid, non-contact 3D surface scanning of biological topologies requiring high repeatability and biocompatible operating parameters.

### 2. Engineering Modeling & Design Focus
The development cycle involved parallel mechanical and electrical optimization tailored strictly to the system's functional requirements:

* **Mechanical Modeling:** Structural analysis, kinematic optimizations, and material selection to minimize deflection, eliminate vibration, and ensure geometric stability during high-speed sensor positioning.
* **Electrical & Control Modeling:** Selection of high-resolution actuators, sensor feedback integration, and motor drive simulations to achieve micrometer-level positional accuracy.

---

## Design Selection and Fabrication Staging
Multiple design iterations were analyzed and validated against structural dynamics, cost constraints, and procurement timelines. The selected final design has been optimized for immediate CNC fabrication, assembly, and physical loop testing.
